Foundation of Atmosphere

Architectural & Perimeter Uplighting

Uplighting is the foundational brushstroke of event lighting. Our fixtures are state-of-the-art wireless RGBAW+UV units that wash walls, columns, and ballroom perimeters in lush tones ranging from warm 2700K champagne candlelight to royal blush and deep indigo.

  • 100% Cable-Free: Battery-powered fixtures eliminate taped wires and messy power drops.
  • True Color Matching: Exact Kelvin temperature matching to harmonize with your floral and linen palette.
  • Dynamic DMX Choreography: Subtle transitions from cocktail hour warmth to dinner elegance, and high-energy party scenes.
Coverage: 1 fixture per 8–10 ft of wall Battery Life: Up to 18 Hours CRI: >95+ High Color Fidelity

Structural Softening & Grandeur

Custom Ceiling & Wall Fabric Draping

Fabric draping completely redefines room proportions. Our master drapery artisans tailor flame-retardant sheer chiffon, crushed velour, and poly-premier fabrics to mask unattractive wall art, lower soaring ceilings for intimate dining, or create theatrical starburst ceiling canopies.

  • Starburst Ceiling Canopies: Multi-point radial draping emanating from a central chandelier or focal floral ring.
  • Perimeter Room Wraps: Floor-to-ceiling 360-degree wall draping to soften sterile hotel ballrooms or industrial warehouses.
  • IFR Safety Certified: Inherently flame-retardant NFPA 701 fabrics approved by local fire marshals.
Fabrics: Sheer Chiffon, Velour, Poly-Premier Heights: Up to 32ft Rigging Heights Rigging: Non-invasive ceiling clamps

Celestial Twinkle & Romance

Fairy Lights, Bistro & Light Curtains

Infuse your venue with thousands of warm, twinkling stars. We design dense starlight ceilings under marquee tents, criss-cross vintage Edison bistro festoons across open courtyards, and build cascading 20-foot light curtains behind the sweetheart table.

  • Dense Sea of Stars: Micro-LED drops woven through sheer fabric for an ethereal fairy-tale canopy.
  • Vintage Bistro Festoons: Warm 2200K amber incandescent filament aesthetic with low-energy LED safety.
  • Full Dimming Control: Easily adjust intensity for dramatic speeches and romantic first dances.
