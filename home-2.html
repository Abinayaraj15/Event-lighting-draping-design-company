The Artisanal Process

How We Build Flawless Fabric Scenography

From initial laser venue survey to on-site handheld steaming and photometric tuning.

01

Laser Elevation Survey

We map your venue ceiling anchor beams, HVAC clearances, and sprinkler drop limits using laser distance meters to calculate exact sag curves.

02

Structural CAD Blueprinting

We generate certified load calculations and rigging vector schematics to ensure 100% compliance with venue fire codes and weight ratings.

03

Non-Invasive Rigging

We use certified beam clamps, magnetic grid mounts, and aircraft wire that never drill, scratch, or leave marks on delicate historic venue plaster.

04

Tension-Tuned Track Fitting

High-tensile crossbar tracks ensure zero center sag or drooping across spans up to 100 feet, maintaining pristine horizontal symmetry.

05

Industrial Steam Pressing

Every single yard of fabric is steam-pressed on-site by our drapery crew after installation so your photographs feature zero transit folds or wrinkles.

06

Precision Floor Puddling

Our artisans hand-dress the base of every drape panel, creating signature 3-to-5 inch fluid fabric puddles that pool gracefully against marble and hardwood.
