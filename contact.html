Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our event lighting and draping process.

How far in advance should we reserve our event date?

We accept bookings up to 18 months in advance. Peak wedding and gala dates (May through October and December) frequently sell out 6 to 9 months prior. We recommend securing your date as soon as your venue contract is finalized.

Are your draping fabrics certified for strict venue fire codes?

Yes, absolutely. 100% of our sheer chiffon, poly-premier, and velour fabrics are Inherently Flame Retardant (IFR) and carry NFPA 701 and California Title 19 certifications. We proactively provide these test certificates to your venue and local fire marshal.

Do you handle setup, live programming, and post-event teardown?

Yes. Every proposal includes full white-glove delivery, structural rigging, color temperature balancing, on-site DMX scene direction throughout your dinner and reception, and complete post-event strike so your venue is left spotless.

Can your fixtures operate without running cords across guest walkways?

Yes. Our entire uplighting, pin-spotting, and accent lighting fleet is 100% wireless and battery-powered with integrated wireless DMX control. This eliminates hazardous cables, taped floors, and ugly power strips from your celebration.

Can we see a 3D visualization of our venue before committing?

Yes! For all full-scale ballroom transformations, our lighting architects build an accurate 3D CAD simulation based on your venue's actual architectural blueprints, allowing you to preview the exact illumination angles and color temperatures in advance.
