01

ETCP Certified Riggers & On-Site Supervision

All overhead hanging activities executed by LUMINA & VEIL are overseen by an ETCP (Entertainment Technician Certification Program) certified Arena or Theatre Rigging Lead. We do not permit general event laborers, floral staff, or outside decorators to attach gear to overhead structural points without certified supervisor approval.

Standard of Structural Responsibility

Under ANSI E1.6-1 and OSHA 1926.251, our rigging directors retain ultimate on-site authority to halt an overhead installation if venue beam conditions, severe weather (for outdoor marquee structures), or point load limits compromise physical safety.

  • Daily Pre-Rig Inspections: All motorized chain hoists, wire rope bridles, and truss pins are visually and mechanically inspected prior to every flight.
  • PPE Enforcement: Hard hats (ANSI Z89.1), fall arrest harnesses, and steel-toe work boots are mandatory during all overhead installation and de-rigging phases.
  • Clear Fall-Zone Protocol: During the flight of heavy fixtures (e.g., crystal chandeliers or box truss grids), a 20-foot ground perimeter is cordoned off from venue staff.
02

Point Load Capacities & Advance CAD Approval

To protect venue architectural heritage and ceiling structural integrity, we adhere to strict load verification schedules:

  • Advance Submission Deadline: Venue management and structural directors must receive our detailed Rigging Plot and Point Load Schedule a minimum of 14 business days prior to event load-in.
  • Vectorworks CAD Plots: Our technical office delivers dimensioned 2D/3D plots indicating exact hang coordinates, trim heights, shackle bridle angles, and dead-hang weights in pounds.
  • Chandelier Dead-Weight Ceilings: Statement chandeliers exceeding 250 lbs require a secondary engineer-approved load point certificate from the venue facility engineering team.
  • Bridle Angle Constraints: Bridle angles exceeding 120° are strictly prohibited to prevent extreme horizontal tension forces on venue I-beams.

Insurance Certification, Load-In & Strike Curfews

We carry comprehensive industry-grade insurance backing to protect venues, planners, and clients against structural liability:

  • Certificate of Insurance (COI): We issue a custom COI within 24 hours naming the venue, management group, and event planning firm as Additional Insured with $5,000,000 General Aggregate and $1,000,000 Per Occurrence coverage.
  • Load-In Timeline Windows: Complex aerial draping and chandelier rigging requires a minimum 4 to 6-hour load-in window prior to florist and catering setup.
  • Quiet Strike & Venue Restoration: Following event conclusion, our crew executes systematic load-out adhering to venue freight elevator weight limits and leaves all walls and beams in pristine condition.
