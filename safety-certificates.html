NFPA 701 Flame Resistance Overview

The National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) Standard 701 is the benchmark flammability test for decorative textiles used in public assembly buildings, wedding ballrooms, and entertainment tents throughout North America.

Zero Fire Risk Guarantee

When exposed to direct open candle flames or theatrical light heat, our textiles do not support combustion. The fabric self-extinguishes immediately upon removal of the ignition source and will not melt into flaming molten droplets.

  • Test Method 2 (Large Scale): Applied to heavy plush velour backdrops, multilayer acoustic drapery, and vinyl room liners hanging in folds or swags longer than 10 feet.
  • California State Fire Marshal Title 19: Additional stringent testing for flame spread and toxic smoke density compliance required across the western United States.

Inherent Flame Resistance (IFR) vs. Topical Chemical Treatment

Many low-cost event rental companies use non-certified fabric sprayed with a topical fire-retardant salt chemical (FR). Such topical sprays wash out during laundering, degrade when exposed to venue humidity, and leave unpleasant chemical odors.

  • True IFR (Inherent Flame Resistance): The flame-retardant polymer is integrated directly into the molecular synthetic yarn extrusion. It cannot be washed out, dry-cleaned away, or degraded by age.
  • Odor-Free & Hypoallergenic: Safe for indoor dinner receptions, delicate bridal bouquets, and sensitive guests.
  • Permanent Accreditation: Our fabrics never require on-site aerosol spray re-treatment, ensuring 100% consistent fire marshal compliance year after year.
04

Municipal Fire Marshal Submission & Clearance Process

Historic theaters, hotel ballrooms, and civic convention centers frequently require an official Fire Marshal Permit prior to installing decorative ceiling draping or marquee swags.

  • Pre-Assembled Document Packet: We prepare a customized Fire Marshal Compliance Packet for your specific venue, including laboratory burn certificates, flammability test lot numbers, and fabric swatch samples.
  • Sprinkler Clearance Adherence: In venues with overhead fire sprinkler systems, all ceiling swags are rigged with a mandatory minimum clearance of 18 inches below sprinkler heads (NFPA 13 standard).
  • Emergency Exit & Sign Visibility: Wall drapery is precision-measured to never obscure illuminated emergency EXIT signs, egress panic hardware, fire alarm strobe units, or manual pull stations.
05

Wireless Battery Uplight & Electrical Safety

Our wireless lighting fleet eliminates hazardous floor cables that present tripping hazards around banquet tables and dance floors.

  • Intelligent BMS Protection: Every wireless RGBAW+UV fixture features an integrated Battery Management System (BMS) with over-current, over-voltage, and thermal runaway auto-shutdown protection.
  • Certified Battery Cells: All lithium-ion power packs carry UL 1642 and UN 38.3 transport safety certifications.
  • IP65 Weatherproof Sealed Enclosures: Outdoor uplights are rated to IP65 standards, completely protected against condensation, wine spills, and heavy rain during garden receptions.
