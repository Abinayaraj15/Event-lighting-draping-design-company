01

VIP Discretion, Celebrity Events & Non-Disclosure Agreements

LUMINA & VEIL regularly designs lighting atmospheres for private estate celebrations, high-profile corporate product summits, and celebrity nuptials where privacy is of paramount importance.

Mandatory Crew Non-Disclosure Sign-Off

Every lighting technician, master rigger, console programmer, and fabric artisan deployed on-site by LUMINA & VEIL is bound by a legally binding Non-Disclosure Agreement. Personal smartphone photography, backstage video recording, and unauthorized social media geotagging are strictly prohibited and subject to immediate dismissal and legal recourse.

  • Strict Embargo Timeline: We never publish portfolio transformations, behind-the-scenes footage, or venue details until our clients and lead wedding planners have granted explicit written release post-event.
  • Private Residence Masking: For events hosted on private residential estates, street addresses, gate entry codes, and perimeter architectural features are never recorded on publicly accessible production servers.
  • Guest Identity Shield: Guest rosters, seating place cards, and VIP security staging schedules shared with our lighting director for cueing purposes are purged within 7 business days following event strike.
02

Information We Collect & Technical Purpose

To engineer custom photometric plots, verify structural load ratings, and execute seamless venue load-ins, we collect minimal and strictly necessary information:

  • Planner & Host Identity: Full names, primary email addresses, direct mobile telephone numbers, and billing entity details for formal proposal delivery.
  • Event Architectural Specifications: Venue name, ballroom room names, ceiling heights, architectural rigging plots, CAD vector files, power distribution box locations, and load-in dock dimensions.
  • Lighting Design Preferences: Selected color temperatures (e.g., 2400K candlelight vs. 3200K champagne gold), floral color palettes, fabric swatch choices (Chiffon, Velour, Organza), and timeline schedules for DMX scene changes.
  • Secure Payment Tokenization: Retainer deposits and balance payments are processed directly through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateway processors. We never store raw credit card numbers or banking account passwords on our local servers.
03

Photography, Transformation Media & Attribution

Lighting and draping are inherently visual crafts. We take tremendous pride in documenting the architectural metamorphosis of empty spaces into breathtaking atmospheric wonderlands.

  • Before & After Portfolio Usage: Our creative media team captures ambient transformation imagery focusing strictly on architectural room shots, fabric ceiling canopies, and lighting fixtures prior to guest arrival.
  • Client Opt-Out Rights: If you or your organization requires 100% complete media silence, you may check the "Private Event / NDA Enforced" box in our design contract. In such cases, zero images or video snippets will be captured or displayed.
  • Collaborative Vendor Credits: When publishing public portfolio galleries, we always provide prominent attribution to fellow creative partners including floral designers, event planners, rental houses, and photographers.
04

CAD Vector Blueprints & Technical Security

Proprietary venue floorplans and technical lighting vector files contain sensitive structural data regarding building beams, emergency exits, and high-voltage electrical drops.

  • Enterprise Encryption: All client project folders, Vectorworks CAD plots, and rigging weight calculation spreadsheets are protected via AES 256-bit encryption in transit and at rest.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Only our Senior Lighting Designers and ETCP Certified Rigging Leads assigned directly to your production have access to your venue files.
  • DMX Lighting Program Security: Custom GrandMA3 and wireless show files created for your evening are archived securely and never repurposed for unauthorized third parties.
05

Third-Party Collaboration & Venue Coordination

We strictly do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or event data with advertisers, third-party marketing brokers, or data analytics aggregators.

Information is shared exclusively with:

  • Accredited Venue Management: Submitting insurance certificates ($5M aggregate liability), load-in vehicle registrations, and certified staff credentials for security clearance.
  • Municipal Fire Marshals: Providing NFPA 701 flammability certificates and flame-resistant fabric compliance documentation when required for municipal permits.
  • Structural Engineering Inspectors: Furnishing ceiling point load calculations and rigging hang-plots when hanging heavy chandeliers or truss canopies.
06

Your Data Rights & Regulatory Compliance

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and international privacy frameworks, you retain comprehensive rights over your personal information:

  • Right to Review: You may request a complete digital export of all contact records, proposals, and design notes associated with your account.
  • Right to Deletion: Following the successful completion of your event and regulatory tax retention periods, you may request the complete deletion of your records from our systems.
  • Non-Discrimination: We do not alter our premium service pricing, technical quality, or artistic dedication based on your exercise of your privacy rights.
